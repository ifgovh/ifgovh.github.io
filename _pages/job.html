Join us

Bring your questions.

We welcome PhD applicants and undergraduate research interns interested in how the brain computes and what it can teach us about artificial intelligence.

Get in touch ↗

What you might work on

Projects connect computational neuroscience, neural dynamics, brain-inspired learning and embodied intelligence. Start with the Research page and choose a question that interests you.

Who should apply

Students from computer science, neuroscience, physics, mathematics, engineering and related backgrounds are welcome. We value curiosity, mathematical reasoning, programming skills and an interest in working across disciplines.

PhD applicants

Please send a CV, academic transcripts, a short statement of your research interests, relevant project or publication links, and the names of potential referees.

Research interns

Please send a CV, transcripts, a short introduction to your interests and relevant experience, and your expected availability.

Start a conversation

A thoughtful introduction helps.

Tell us which scientific question draws you to the group and how your background connects to it.
