--- layout: academic title: Research permalink: /research/ section: research description: "Our lab approaches biological and artificial intelligence as a unified problem in NeuroAI. Rather than treating circuit anatomy, population dynamics, and behavioral function as separate topics, we investigate Architecture ↔ Dynamics ↔ Function as one coupled dynamical system across computer science, neuroscience, physics, and mathematics. Mechanistic models of cortical microcircuits guide the design of brain-inspired and brain-like intelligent architectures, while spectral, statistical-physical, and generative machine learning tools help decode how biological brains compute and learn." ---
